The work the demo didn’t show

Production costsWhat happens to the bill when usage grows?
MaintenanceWho keeps the agents and their infrastructure working?
CapacityWhat existing work stops so the team has room?
OwnershipWho runs the result after the pilot?

The technology is for sale
A way of working is not

Make responsibilities visible

  • Each agent has a person responsible for it
  • It is on one company-wide list
  • It passes a trial before it meets customers
  • Its costs are visible to the team that runs it
  • Teams use a shared rulebook and common components

What nobody has settled yet

This is the middle of the change, and three questions are open

How much can run without a human checking?StrongDM ships code nobody reads, while Amazon requires senior sign-off after outages
What does it cost?Uber used up its full-year AI coding budget in four months
How big should a team be, and what happens to roles?Some companies shrank teams because of AI and later rebuilt them
